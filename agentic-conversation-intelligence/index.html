A working definition

More than transcription. More than a reactive answer.

Agentic Conversation Intelligence combines the live Discussion, the Outcome you set, and the Session Context you select to help determine what may be useful next.

Understand

Follow the conversation in context

ThoughtWaves uses the active Discussion and the information you deliberately provide for the session. It is designed for video calls, online meetings, business calls, and in-person professional discussions supported by your configured audio setup.

Evaluate

Consider value before interrupting

TW Intelligence Assist evaluates whether a question, clarification, risk, connection, challenge, or next step appears useful enough to surface at that moment.

Support

Keep the professional in control

Suggestions remain assistance—not guaranteed answers or autonomous decisions. You decide what to ask, say, challenge, or carry into the next conversation.

How it works

Outcome-driven conversational intelligence across the full session.

Instead of treating every meeting as an isolated transcript, ThoughtWaves connects preparation, live assistance, and review around what you are trying to accomplish.

BeforeOutcome → Influence → Session Context

Define the result you want, how strongly guidance should support it, and which notes, documents, presentations, spreadsheets, images, or entered text should ground the session.

DuringDiscussion · Ask TW · Assist · TW Intelligence Assist

Follow the shared timeline, ask directly, request help continuing, or consider proactive contextual assistance when the conversation indicates it may add value.

AfterReview → Download → Summarize

Review decisions, signals, missed points, and supported next actions, then preserve or summarize the material you choose.

Context on your terms

Bring the right information into the current conversation.

User-controlled Session Context lets you select the material that matters instead of asking a general AI assistant or AI meeting copilot to work from the transcript alone.

Session Context

Add supported notes, documents, presentations, spreadsheets, images, and text. Context remains focused on the active session unless you deliberately carry material forward.

Live Discussion

Selective intelligence

A real-time AI assistant can create distraction if it surfaces every possible answer. ThoughtWaves can selectively surface a useful point when it may add value—and stay quiet when it may not.

Frequently asked questions

Agentic Conversation Intelligence, in practical terms.

Is ThoughtWaves an AI meeting assistant?

Yes. It can support meetings and calls, but its scope extends beyond notes: it connects preparation, user-selected context, live assistance, proactive intelligence, and post-session review around an Outcome.

Does ThoughtWaves join as a meeting bot?

No. The ThoughtWaves desktop app runs alongside the conversation and does not appear as another participant to invite.

Does it always know the right answer or question?

No. ThoughtWaves suggests, surfaces, and helps you consider information based on available context. Its guidance can be incomplete or incorrect, and the user remains responsible for what to say and do.

Does it replace meeting notes?

No. Transcripts, summaries, and downloads can support the workflow, but the product is designed to help before and during the conversation as well as afterward.

Is visual context always active?

No. Visual context is optional and user-controlled. Its availability and provider-processing boundaries depend on the current feature and configuration.

What is available in the current beta?

The public beta supports Windows and macOS workflows described on this site, including session preparation, live Discussion, Ask TW, Assist, TW Intelligence Assist, review, downloads, summaries, and user-selected Session Context. Capabilities not described as part of the current beta should not be assumed available.
