02 · During Meeting

Stay with the conversation.

Use the level of help that fits the moment—from following the shared Discussion to asking directly or considering a suggested next direction.

Discussion

The live shared conversational timeline.

Keep the current conversation visible as context develops.

Ask TW

“I already have a question.”

Ask explicitly to clarify the discussion, use Session Context, get a technical explanation, or request a suggested response.

Assist

“Help me continue from here.”

Continue from the recent conversation to suggest a useful question or follow-up, identify what to clarify or raise next, or surface something that may have been missed.

TW Intelligence Assist

“Surface what I may need without me asking.”

ThoughtWaveAI can proactively surface a useful question, clarification, challenge, or point when the discussion, Session Context, and user goals indicate it may help.

Session Context

Your information, focused on this session.

Give ThoughtWaveAI relevant information before or during a conversation. Session Context is user-controlled, scoped to the active session, and available across its desktop and Mobile Companion experience.

Mobile Companion

The same active session, on a convenient second screen.

Mobile Companion is a lightweight browser extension of the current Windows desktop session—not a separate mobile product or cloud-synced session.

  • View the same Discussion
  • Open and add Session Context
  • Trigger Assist without leaving the timeline
  • Ask TW from the persistent bottom composer

Context supports Take Photo, Choose Photo, Choose File, and manual text entry. Mobile video and standalone mobile apps are not represented.
