Privacy & control

No meeting bot joins your call.

ThoughtWaveAI works alongside the conversation without appearing as another participant. Its processing boundaries depend on the features and providers you choose.

01

No bot participant

ThoughtWaveAI runs alongside your conversation on the Windows desktop, so there is no visible AI participant to invite.

02

Audio and conversation processing

ThoughtWaveAI uses the audio sources you explicitly enable for the session. Device, headset, operating-system, and meeting-app behavior can vary, so users should confirm their audio setup before an important conversation.

03

Local processing

Audio and speech recognition can be processed locally when using a supported local configuration. This does not mean every feature is fully local.

04

AI providers

When an AI feature uses your selected provider, the request may include recent conversation content and relevant user-selected Session Context needed to produce the response. Provider credentials remain part of the desktop configuration and are not presented as Mobile Companion content.

05

Session Context

You choose the notes, text, images, and supported documents added to the active session. They ground relevant intelligence for that session unless you deliberately carry material forward.

06

Mobile Companion

Mobile Companion connects to the active desktop session over the available local connection. It presents the same Discussion, Session Context, Assist, and Ask TW context; it is not described as a separate cloud-synchronized mobile account or standalone app.

07

Logging and diagnostics

Production diagnostics are designed to favor operational metadata over raw conversation content. Users should still treat diagnostic exports and session outputs as potentially sensitive information.

08

Your responsibility

Use recording and AI assistance in accordance with applicable laws, organizational policies, and consent requirements. This page describes product behavior at a high level and is not legal advice.
