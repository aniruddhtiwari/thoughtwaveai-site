Privacy & control

No meeting bot joins your call.

ThoughtWaveAI works alongside the conversation without appearing as another participant. Its processing boundaries depend on the features and providers you choose.

01

No bot participant

ThoughtWaveAI runs alongside your conversation on the Windows desktop, so there is no visible AI participant to invite.

02

Audio and conversation processing

ThoughtWaveAI processes the audio sources available to the active session based on your configuration. Device, headset, operating-system, and meeting-app behavior can vary, so confirm your audio setup before an important conversation.

03

Local processing

Audio and speech recognition can be processed locally when using a supported local configuration. This does not mean every feature is fully local.

04

AI providers

When an AI feature uses your selected provider, the request may include recent conversation content and relevant user-selected Session Context needed to produce the response. This provider processing is separate from ThoughtWaveAI product telemetry. Provider credentials remain part of the desktop configuration and are not presented as Mobile Companion content.

05

Session Context

You choose the notes, text, images, and supported documents added to the active session. ThoughtWaveAI does not need to accumulate every prior meeting automatically; you decide what material to carry into a future Session Context.

06

Mobile Companion

Mobile Companion provides lightweight access to the same active desktop session over the available local connection, including Discussion, Session Context, Assist, and Ask TW. It is not a standalone phone app or cloud-synchronized account.

07

Diagnostics and product telemetry

ThoughtWaveAI may send limited operational telemetry needed to understand product usage, reliability, and onboarding progress. This can include account email, an installation and session identifier, app and operating-system information, session duration, feature-use counts, aggregate response timing, Session Context count and file type, and non-content error codes. ThoughtWaveAI telemetry does not automatically send meeting transcripts, audio, Session Context contents, Ask TW questions, AI responses, screenshots, filenames, local file paths, or provider API keys.

08

Session feedback

If you submit Session Feedback, ThoughtWaveAI stores the rating, selected feedback tags, and any optional comment you enter, along with limited session and device metadata. Because free-text feedback is user-provided, avoid including confidential or sensitive meeting content unless you intend to submit it.

09

Your responsibility

Use recording and AI assistance in accordance with applicable laws, organizational policies, and consent requirements. This page describes product behavior at a high level and is not legal advice.
